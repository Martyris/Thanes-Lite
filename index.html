The Gear Advisor — every slot, what's best at your level, and where to get it
What it does

Gear that fits your build, not someone else's

Pick a class and a build, enter your gear (or let the addon sync it) and Thanes ranks every item you can get at your level for that build: dungeon drops, quest rewards, crafted gear and world drops. Each slot shows what you wear, the best upgrade, what opens up in the next few levels, and exactly where each item comes from.

Gear you wear with a random bonus is scored with its real stats (the addon reads them), set bonuses count when a piece completes a set, and items you can't use yet — the wrong profession, a quest out of reach — say why.

A levelling route, step by step, on the map

The Quest Guide plans your route through a zone: which quests to pick up together, which objectives to do in one trip, where to hand them in, and which follow-ups that unlocks. It weighs XP and reward upgrades, follows chains that lead somewhere worth going, and picks up dungeon quests before you get there.

Every step is drawn on the zone map with its quest areas, and copies a /way waypoint for TomTom. With the addon, your route follows you in game.

Talent builds for every class — Talented perk included

Builds for all nine classes, researched from the community's guides, drawn on WoW: Forever's own talent trees with the game's tooltips. Thanes counts the points you have at your level, including the extra points from the Talented legacy perk.

Dungeons

The dungeons around your level, recommended by the upgrades they drop for your build, their quest XP and how well they fit — with boss loot and drop chances, and a checklist for your plan.

Professions

The cheapest way from your skill to the next tier, priced from your own Auctionator scans, with a shopping list, the trainer tiers and which recipes you already know.

Gold making

Farm spots around your level ranked by gold an hour, what your professions can sell, and tips for your class — all from what's really listed when you scan.

The companion addon

ThanesSyncLite

Optional, and small. It saves your character for Thanes Lite and brings your quest route into the game.

  • Syncs your level, worn gear (random bonuses included), professions and recipes, completed quests and quest log — type /thanes or click the Thanes button.
  • Shows your route in game: the next step with its quests and progress, moving on by itself as you complete it, with a TomTom arrow pointing the way.

Thanes Lite can install it for you, or get it from CurseForge or the latest release.

How it was made

Built with Claude Code

Thanes was written by Martyris working with Claude Code, Anthropic's agentic coding tool — the gear engine, the quest router, the talent trees, the profession routes and the in-game addon.

The ideas, the priorities and the playtesting came from playing WoW: Forever every day: what was missing, what felt wrong, what would actually help on the way to level 30.

Game data is read from your own WoW: Forever install — icons, maps and interface art are never shipped with the app. Quest and item data comes from the community projects credited below.

Thanes Lite is free. It's unofficial and has no connection to Blizzard Entertainment. If it helps you, you can support what Martyris makes on Patreon.

Get Thanes Lite

Download the installer and run it: Thanes Lite installs for your Windows user, makes a shortcut, and keeps itself up to date — new versions install with one click.

On first start, one click downloads the quest and item data (about half a minute), and Thanes Lite finds your WoW: Forever install by itself.

What you need
