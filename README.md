# Thanes Lite

A free Windows companion app for **World of Warcraft: Forever**, and its optional in-game addon **ThanesSyncLite**.

- Characters with builds for all nine classes, a Gear Advisor laid out like the character window, best in slot gear and
  upgrades, stats and talent trees
- A Quest Guide with a levelling route, zone maps, quest chains and dungeon prep, plus quest and item lookups
- Dungeons with boss loot and drop chances, professions with levelling routes, and auction house prices from your own
  Auctionator scans
- **ThanesSyncLite** (optional) syncs your level, gear, professions and quests to the app, and shows your quest route in
  game with TomTom arrows

## Download

Get `ThanesLite-Setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Martyris/Thanes-Lite/releases/latest). Thanes
Lite updates itself from there. The addon is in the same release (`ThanesSyncLite-<version>.zip`), and the app can also
install it for you.
